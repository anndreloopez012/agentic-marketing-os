# Deliverable Contracts

Use this file to shape outputs into consistent professional artifacts.

## Brand Diagnosis

Must include:
- what the brand sells
- who buys
- problem solved
- promise
- differentiator
- objections
- tone
- opportunities

## Buyer Persona

Must include:
- context
- pain
- desire
- objections
- content behavior
- purchase trigger
- message that converts

## Content Calendar

Must include:
- date or cadence
- pillar
- format
- hook
- CTA
- objective
- funnel stage

## Campaign Plan

Must include:
- objective
- audience
- offer
- promise
- angles
- channel breakdown
- CTA path
- metrics
- test plan

## Landing Copy

Must include:
- hero
- subheadline
- problem
- promise
- proof or proof gap
- CTA
- section structure

## Commercial Deck

Must include:
- problem framing
- who the brand is
- offer architecture
- differentiators
- expected value
- CTA / next step

## Audit / Review

Must include:
- what works
- what is weak
- why it matters
- recommended fix
- priority
