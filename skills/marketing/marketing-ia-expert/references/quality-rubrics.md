# Marketing Quality Rubrics

Use for reviewing, improving, or grading marketing work.

## Strategy Quality

Score 1-5:
- Specific business context.
- Clear audience and pain.
- Clear promise and differentiator.
- Channel fit.
- Realistic next action.

## Content Quality

Score 1-5:
- Hook is specific and relevant.
- Content solves a real user problem.
- Tone matches brand and channel.
- CTA is clear and not forced.
- Piece maps to a funnel stage.

## Campaign Quality

Score 1-5:
- Objective is measurable.
- Offer is clear.
- Message angles cover pain, desire, objection and proof.
- Content sequence is coherent.
- Metrics and test plan are defined.

## AI Output Review

Reject or revise if:
- It is generic.
- It invents facts, numbers, guarantees or testimonials.
- It uses exaggerated claims.
- It ignores channel constraints.
- It lacks CTA or deliverable format.
- It sounds robotic or over-polished.

## Professional Upgrade Checklist

- Add audience specificity.
- Add concrete examples.
- Add objections and responses.
- Add funnel stage.
- Add format and CTA.
- Add measurement.
- Add assumptions where data is missing.
